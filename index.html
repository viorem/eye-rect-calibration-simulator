Reading it

Vertical axis is how far the eye-rect midpoint may sit from frame centre (540) before the gate fails. Horizontal axis is eRect_width — further right means the face is closer to the camera.

As shipped sits asymmetrically around zero — more room to drift left than right at every distance — and narrows to a point as the face approaches the frame width.

As likely intended (dashed) is the same formula with the left bound mirrored, × (1 − r). It is symmetric, but stricter than the shipped build, and it still collapses with closeness.

Proposed and production (X) are both flat and symmetric — production's is ratio × 540, independent of width. So the shipped Kotlin already decouples centring from closeness. It differs mainly in having no containment check; the proposal adds the frame-edge taper.
